# 10 Publishable Memo
## Summary
IBM remains a watchlist name pending cleaner evidence that earnings quality and cash conversion support the AI and hybrid-cloud narrative.
## Business
IBM is repositioning around software, Red Hat, automation, and AI infrastructure.
## Thesis
The bull case depends on durable software-led growth and better validation of the cash earnings bridge.
## Valuation
The archived base-case IV remains materially above the current price, but confidence is constrained by accounting-quality questions.
## Risks
Weak OCF conversion, consulting margin pressure, and debt-funded M&A remain the core risks.
## Catalysts
Next earnings, Red Hat growth tracking, and better disclosure on earnings quality.
